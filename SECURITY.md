# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security issues **privately**. Do not open a public issue, pull request or discussion.

1. Go to the affected repository's **Security** tab.
2. Choose **Report a vulnerability** to open a private advisory.
3. If private reporting is not available, email **admin@graviton.xyz** with the subject line `SECURITY`.

Include what is affected, steps to reproduce, and the impact you expect.

## What happens next

- We confirm receipt within 3 business days.
- We share an assessment and planned fix timeline within 10 business days.
- We credit reporters in the advisory unless you ask us not to.

Please give us reasonable time to fix an issue before you disclose it publicly.

## Scope

Code published in the gazeprotocol organization. Third-party dependencies should be reported to their maintainers,
and to us if they affect Gaze.
