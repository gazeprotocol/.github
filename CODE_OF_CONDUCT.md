# Code of Conduct

We want the Gaze community to be welcoming, respectful and useful to everyone who takes part,
regardless of background, identity, experience or ability.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## What we expect

- Be respectful of differing views and experiences.
- Give and accept feedback with care.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Promoting tokens, prices or trades in project spaces.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in every Gaze project space, including repositories, discussions, community calls and chat,
and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report concerns to **admin@graviton.xyz**. Reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code,
and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone whose conduct is harmful.
Responses follow the Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines: correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.
